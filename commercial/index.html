Safe Installation

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to provide safe conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected water heater can lead to carbon monoxide hazards from bad venting, scalding at the tap, Legionella growth in water stored too cool, and costly water damage from a failed tank. Clearwater Water Heater provides commercial installations and inspections that cover venting and combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve and discharge line routing, expansion tanks, thermostatic mixing valves, drain pans, seismic strapping where required, and permits and inspections. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

The paperwork matters as much as the repair. Every account gets a documentation binder with detailed service logs, temperature readings, and relief valve test dates, so you can demonstrate due diligence to landlords, insurers, and inspectors. Heavy-use equipment, in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms, gets a more frequent service schedule.

After-Hours Service

Fast Commercial Water Heater Service

A hot water failure on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, cancel a day of salon appointments, leave hotel guests with cold showers, and expose the owner to tenant disputes. Clearwater Water Heater provides fast emergency water heater service for commercial properties throughout Clearwater and surrounding areas, and we schedule non-urgent work after hours or before opening so your doors stay open.

Commercial emergencies come in many forms. The cause could be a failed gas valve, a burned-out element, a flame sensor that stopped proving flame, a tripped high-limit switch, a leaking tank, a stalled recirculation pump, or a tankless unit throwing error codes. Whatever it is, we find it fast, get hot water flowing again, and record every step for your facility files. Then we put maintenance in place so the same failure doesn't return.

Preventive Care

Proactive Water Heater Care for Businesses

A maintenance program from Clearwater Water Heater gets built around your property's specific equipment, how hard the system works, and your budget. Visits include tank flushing and anode checks, tankless descaling, relief valve testing, burner, flue, and gas valve inspection, recirculation pump and mixing valve checks, and temperature verification at key fixtures.
